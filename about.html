About Moldrivo

A studio built to outcraft the giants

We believe small, senior, design-led teams ship better products than large agencies — and we've built Moldrivo to prove it every single day.

Our story

From two laptops to global launches

Moldrivo started with a simple frustration: most agencies overcharge, overpromise and under-deliver. We wanted the opposite — honest scoping, world-class craft and senior people actually doing the work.

Today we're a six-person team spanning design, engineering and AI, trusted by startups and established companies on three continents. We stay deliberately small — because it's how we keep quality at Apple-level and relationships at partnership level.

Core values

The principles we never compromise

Craft over speed

We never ship "good enough". Every detail gets the same obsessive treatment, whether it's a landing page or an AI system.

Radical honesty

If we can't deliver on time or a scope doesn't make sense, we tell you early — never after the invoice.

AI-first thinking

We look for the intelligent way to solve every problem — automating the repetitive so humans can focus on what matters.

Total transparency

Weekly demos, open boards and milestone-based billing. You always see exactly where your project stands.

Ship & iterate

Perfect is the enemy of shipped. We launch working products fast, then compound improvements with real data.

Clients first

Your business goals are the definition of done. We measure success in your metrics, not our deliverables.
